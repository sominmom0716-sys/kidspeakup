    How to practise (연습 방법)

    • Read the situation, then say the phrase out loud before you tap the card to flip it. Tap the speaker to hear it.
    • Calm voice, eyes up, hands down. Say it once, then walk away or go back to what she was doing.
    • When kids leave her out, practise a relaxed shrug and a calm “Okay, whatever!” face. Staying calm keeps the power with her.
    • Tick the Checklist when she says a phrase in a real moment or in role-play at home. Celebrate trying, not perfect English.
